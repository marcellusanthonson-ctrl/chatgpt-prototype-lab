# ECR Capability 001

ECR is a proposed transversal evidential-control and reconciliation capability. It is documentation, not software.

Its purpose is to preserve provenance, authority, applicability, contradiction, claim lineage and supersession before a consumer acts on evidence. LAB remains the governance owner; Symphonie is the first intended consumer through a future documented interface.

ECR does not resolve RAG, select retrieval technology, choose a provider or architecture, implement runtime, access repositories automatically, integrate with products or grant authority. Architecture remains blocked until the gates in `MANIFEST.json` are independently satisfied and Jonathan Martínez records a separate decision and authorization.
